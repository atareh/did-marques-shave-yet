01The bet

When Tesla showed off its two-seat Cybercab in October 2024, Elon Musk said it would cost under $30,000 and be on sale before 2027. Marques Brownlee didn't buy it, and put his hair on the line.

    The Cybercab: two seats, no steering wheel, no pedals.

  2. 02

    The price

    $30,000 or less.

  3. 03

    The catch

    It has to ship to regular customers before the end of 2026.
